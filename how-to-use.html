How to Use FestiBites

A simple guide to finding a recipe and using the kitchen tools.

1

Find a recipe

Type a recipe name, ingredient or food category in the search box, then tap Search. You can also choose a category chip to browse that kind of food. Tap Surprise Me if you want FestiBites to pick a recipe for you.

2

Open and prepare

Tap View full recipe to see the ingredients, cooking time, servings and difficulty. Read all the steps first, check that you have the ingredients, and ask an adult for help with knives, hot pans, boiling water or frying.

3

Adjust servings

Use the minus and plus buttons beside Custom servings, or enter a number. Ingredient amounts that FestiBites can recognize will adjust automatically. Small pinches, salt and seasonings may still need to be adjusted to taste.

4

Follow each step

Follow the numbered instructions from top to bottom. Tap a step to mark it as completed while you cook. Read the whole step before starting it, and check that food is properly cooked before serving.

5

Share or print

Tap the share icon to preview the recipe, copy its text or share it using an available app. Tap the printer icon to open a print view, if your browser supports it. From the print screen, you may be able to choose Save as PDF.

6

Browse more

Tap Show More Recipes to load more cards. Use the category filters to narrow your choices, or return to All to browse the full collection.

Beginner cooking tip: Wash your hands, clean your work area, measure ingredients before cooking, and follow food-safety guidance. Cooking times can vary with your stove, cookware and ingredient size.
